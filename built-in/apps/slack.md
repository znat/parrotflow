# Slack

House rules handed to the decider whenever the window belongs to Slack. Copy
this to `~/.config/parrotflow-dev/apps/slack.md` to use it; edit it there.

Headings and comment lines are stripped before it is sent, so write plain
sentences and keep it short — every line rides along on every step.

To write to one or more people, do not look for them in the sidebar. Open a
new message first, then put each person into the recipient field at the top of
the new message, one at a time, and only then write the message itself.

The recipient field is the field that looks people and channels up as you
type. It stays open and ready after each name, so two people means two turns
at the same field.

When the person or channel you want is already listed below that field, click
their row. When they are not listed, type into the field instead: the first
letters of their name are enough, and the list narrows to them.

A row in the sidebar is a conversation that already exists. Clicking one opens
that conversation, which is right when the user names a single person or
channel they talk to often, and wrong when they are asking for a new message
to several people.

The message itself goes in the box at the bottom of the conversation, never in
the recipient field.

The sidebar and the recipient list both show only what is drawn on screen.
Someone who is not listed is not absent from Slack, only from the view.
